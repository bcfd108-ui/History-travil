North Carolina pilot — closes the state's last two first-photo gaps:
- Greensboro Sit-Ins / Woolworth (2 photos): Carol M. Highsmith exterior of the F.W. Woolworth building, now the International Civil Rights Center & Museum (LOC LC-DIG-highsm-43722), and the restored lunch counter in place (LC-DIG-highsm-43828). No known restrictions on publication. The LoC dates are photographer-provided; no capture year prints. The counter caption names the place and states the museum does not permit gallery photography.

Desk ruling: where nothing of the place survives above ground, the memorial marker on the ground satisfies the first-photo gate, captioned plainly as the marker. No capture year.

Illinois — two new entries, each with its first photo (the Nov 22 photo gate: no entry publishes without one):
- Lincoln Home National Historic Site (new entry): the Lincoln Home in 1860, Meserve Lincoln Photograph No. 16 — the August 8, 1860 procession in front of the house, Lincoln the tall figure in white at the right of the doorway. Public domain (UIUC holding record: "No Copyright - United States"; corroborated at Commons for the LoC copy, LC-USZ62-13682). Credit follows the UIUC holding page as the dossier's §7 prescribes. The halftone's printed "probably in the 1858 campaign" caption is flagged [sic] by LoC and is not followed; the year 1860 prints.
- Starved Rock and the site of Fort St. Louis / Le Rocher (new entry): "Illinois River from Starved Rock," c. 1914, LoC Panoramic Photograph Collection (LC-USZ62-123505), public domain. The photo is the view FROM the summit — the prospect the fort commanded — and the caption says so; it is not a view of the rock. The archival copy-negative's film borders were cropped for display per the source note; the photograph itself is uncropped.
