# Mississippi First Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** First checklist of significant visitable Mississippi historic ground. No Mississippi dossiers exist yet — this is the list the research lane works down when the state opens.
**Counts:** 104 candidates — 94 LEAD, 10 NO-ANCHOR?
**Lead sources used (leads only):** Mississippi Department of Archives & History (MDAH) managed destinations and public-lands listings; National Historic Landmarks in Mississippi (40 NHLs, NPS/Wikipedia list); National Park Service units in Mississippi (Vicksburg NMP, Natchez NHP, Natchez Trace Parkway, Gulf Islands NS, Tupelo NB, Brices Cross Roads NBS, Medgar & Myrlie Evers Home NM); Mississippi Blues Trail / Freedom Trail topic leads; state park and municipal museum listings.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts.

**Research-lane notes:** Mississippi opens most naturally at Natchez (27 candidates in one walkable cluster, 13 NHLs in Adams County alone) and Vicksburg, then the Delta blues/civil-rights corridor. The NO-ANCHOR? rows concentrate where the ground is heaviest — Till sites, battlefields in private hands, and tribal sacred ground at Nanih Waiya, which stays flagged and unrouted unless and until the Choctaw voice leads. MDAH site statuses and small-museum hours shift; the researcher verifies the current gate from primary sources for every LEAD.
