# Alaska First Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** First checklist of significant visitable Alaska historic ground. No Alaska dossiers exist yet — the live catalog (550 entries, 21 states) was checked November 20, 2026 and contains zero Alaska entries, so there are no HAVE marks; this is the list the research lane works down when the state opens.
**Counts:** 119 candidates — 92 on the road system (77 LEAD, 15 NO-ANCHOR?) and 27 held OUT-OF-SCOPE-FOR-NOW (fly-in / bush / marine-highway). Theme flags: 15 ⚑ MILITARY, 3 ⚑ PRISON/LAW (the prison family is thin on Alaska road ground and runs largely through law-enforcement and capital-city records).
**Lead sources used (leads only):** National Register of Historic Places listings for Alaska (420+ properties) and the 50 Alaska National Historic Landmarks (NPS NHL program list); National Park Service Alaska units (Klondike Gold Rush NHP, Wrangell–St. Elias, Denali, Kenai Fjords, Yukon–Charley Rivers, Sitka NHP); Alaska State Parks historic sites (Independence Mine, Big Delta, Fort Abercrombie, Old Sitka); Alaska DNR / DOT highway corridor guides (Alaska, Richardson, Glenn, Parks, Steese, Taylor, Dalton, Haines, Klondike highways); municipal and tribal cultural-center listings; Wikipedia Alaska history and NRHP-in-Alaska lists used as a finder only.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Road-trip framing (owner's ruling):** On Old Ground is a road-trip reference, and Alaska is weighted accordingly. The in-scope list is ground reachable by the road system — the Alaska Highway corridor, Anchorage / Mat-Su / Kenai, the Parks, Richardson, Glenn, Steese, Taylor, and Dalton highways, Valdez, and Haines / Skagway / Hyder (road access through Canada). Fly-in, bush, and marine-highway-only ground is NOT marked NO-ANCHOR — it is held at the end as OUT-OF-SCOPE-FOR-NOW, the same posture as the Hawaii variant ruling: real ground, wrong vehicle. Hawaii itself is excluded entirely by the owner's ruling and does not appear here.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts. OUT-OF-SCOPE-FOR-NOW = fly-in / bush / ferry-only ground, held out of the road-trip core. **THEME flags:** ⚑ MILITARY and ⚑ PRISON/LAW mark the theme-sweep families. Alaska Native ground is flagged with a care note throughout: tribal voice first, village ground is living community ground, and several rows advance only on the tribe's own terms.
