# Michigan & Wisconsin First Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** First checklist of significant visitable Michigan and Wisconsin historic ground. Verified against the live catalog (~/workspace/history-travel/hosted-app/data/sites.json, 550 entries) on this date: **zero Michigan and zero Wisconsin entries exist** — every candidate below is new ground, none marked HAVE.
**Counts:** 175 candidates — 159 LEAD, 16 NO-ANCHOR? (Michigan 88: 81 LEAD, 7 NO-ANCHOR? · Wisconsin 87: 78 LEAD, 9 NO-ANCHOR?)
**Lead sources used (leads only):** National Register of Historic Places listings for Michigan and Wisconsin (incl. National Historic Landmarks); Michigan State Historic Sites register / Michigan SHPO; Mackinac State Historic Parks; Michigan DNR state park historic units; Wisconsin Historical Society's 11 state historic sites; Wisconsin State Register / Architecture & History Inventory (WisHistory); NPS unit lists (Keweenaw NHP, Pictured Rocks, Sleeping Bear Dunes, Apostle Islands, River Raisin); NOAA Thunder Bay National Marine Sanctuary; Michigan lighthouse/maritime museum listings; state mining, logging, and labor topic leads.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts. **★ THEME** = prison / asylum / military family — these count double in the sweep and are flagged wherever they appear.
