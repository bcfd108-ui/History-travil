This is the list the research lanes work down when these states open.

**Lead sources used (leads only):** National Historic Landmark lists for AL (38), TN (31+1), KY (32–33) via NPS/Wikipedia finders; Alabama Historical Commission site holdings (Alabama Code §41-9-250 and AHC properties); Tennessee State Parks / State Historic Sites and NPS Tennessee units; Kentucky Heritage Council landmark and Civil War site lists, Kentucky State Parks historic sites, NPS Kentucky units; U.S. Civil Rights Trail topic leads; institutional-history finders for the prison/asylum rows.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts. **★ = theme family** (prison / asylum / hospital / military) — these count double in the sweep weighting.

## Sequencing notes

- **Alabama opens most naturally in two clusters:** the Birmingham–Montgomery–Selma civil rights corridor (the densest run of NHL ground in the state) and the Tennessee Valley (mounds, Muscle Shoals, space). Old Cahawba and Fort Toulouse give the Black Belt its deep-time pair.
- **Tennessee opens at Memphis and Nashville** — the two NHL-dense metros — with the East Tennessee state-site chain (Sycamore Shoals → Rocky Mount → Chester Inn → Johnson NHS) as the strongest single road line in the three states.
- **Kentucky opens on the Bluegrass line** (Lexington–Harrodsburg–Danville–Frankfort–Bardstown), then Louisville. Camp Nelson and Perryville anchor the Civil War layer; Mammoth Cave is the state's single biggest Indigenous-plus-history draw.
- **Thinnest coverage to strengthen next round:** Alabama's Wiregrass and southwest interior (Dothan–Enterprise), Tennessee's Cumberland Plateau towns beyond Rugby/York, and Kentucky's Jackson Purchase beyond Wickliffe/Paducah — each wants its own county-level enumeration before dossiers are drawn.
