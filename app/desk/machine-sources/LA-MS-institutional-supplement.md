**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Supplementary checklist closing a gap the desk audit found: the Louisiana completion sweep and the Mississippi first sweep contain **zero prison or asylum rows** between them. This file enumerates the institutional ground both lists missed — prisons, asylums, state hospitals, sanatoriums, colony schools, and historic hospitals — for Louisiana and Mississippi only.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Collision-checked against:** the live catalog (550 entries — none in Louisiana or Mississippi yet), `LA-completion-sweep.md`, `MS-first-sweep.md`, and `LA-MS-theme-rows-confirmed.md`. **Already covered — excluded to avoid duplication:** the Taborian Hospital (rides inside the Mound Bayou row of the Mississippi list) and the Amory Regional Museum (a former hospital building, already a Mississippi list row). **Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). HAVE-LEAD = another lane already holds this lead; do not duplicate. NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts. Active prisons and occupied state hospitals are NO-ANCHOR? by rule unless a museum or public anchor exists.
