**Tier and reading policy.** Tier 1 requires marquee evidence. Tier 2 uses a documented national designation or comparable destination signal. Tier 3 uses state significance, public interpretation or a documented regional signal. Tier 4 covers local or obscure ground with limited evidence of reach. A designation or museum is an evidence signal, never an invented visitor count. All incarceration, institutional and memorial ground here is for historical reading or witnessing. No game scoring. Free evidence sources carry this sheet. No photos adopted. Historical photos require inspection, public-domain verification and a printed credit. Coverage uses 49 drivable states with Hawaii tracked separately.
