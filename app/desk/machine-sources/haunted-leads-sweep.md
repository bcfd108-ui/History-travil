# Haunted-History Lead Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Checklist of haunted-history ground harvested from haunted-place directories and ghost-tour operators, so the research lane can work the lore layer down a list instead of building one. Haunted directories and tour operators are used as LEAD FINDERS ONLY — the same discipline as Clio/HMdb/Wikipedia: harvest the places, then research each from primary and independent sources.
**Counts:** 630 candidates — 506 LEAD, 42 NO-ANCHOR?, 75 LISTED (already in an existing sweep list), 7 HAVE (already in the live catalog).

**LORE LAW:** a ghost claim is never a fact. Every place below is a lead for a documented record — prison, asylum, hospital, hotel, battlefield, house — with the haunting handled in the eventual dossier as labeled lore over that record. Nothing here is researched copy; no wording is taken from any lead source.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor appears to exist (a running ghost tour counts as an anchor; anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = documented ground but no lawful public anchor found — flagged, not dropped; never presented as visitable. LISTED = already a lead in a named sweep list (not duplicated here as new). HAVE = already in the live catalog.

**Sources mined (leads only):** hauntedrooms.com state guides (the richest single vein — per-state lists with visiting status); hauntedplaces.org parish/city pages; ghost-tour operators and their stop lists (US Ghost Adventures; Ghosts & Gravestones, Key West; destinationghost.com, St. Augustine; Seattle and Portland ghost tours via GetYourGuide); state and regional roundups (explorelouisiana.com; shreveporttimes.com; visitmo.com; traveliowa.com; iowapbs.org; travelnevada.com; familydestinationsguide.com; thehorrorcollection.com Colorado list; grunge.com / outdooryak.com / familyhandyman.com most-haunted-per-state lists; historic-haunts.net state picks; hellhorror.com; ghostindex.app; Paranormal Northwest and The Midnight Drive podcast episode indexes for the Pacific Northwest).

**Collision check:** every row was checked against the live catalog (550 entries, sites.json) and all ten existing sweep lists plus the LA-MS institutional supplement and theme-row confirmation. Catalog matching is by place name against entry titles/slugs; thematic catalog titles mean a HAVE can hide — the dossier stage re-checks before anything is written.
