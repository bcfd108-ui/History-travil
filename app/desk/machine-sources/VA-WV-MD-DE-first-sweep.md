# Virginia · West Virginia · Maryland · Delaware First Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** First checklist of significant visitable historic ground in Virginia, West Virginia, Maryland, and Delaware. The live catalog (550 entries, 21 states) was checked on this date — it holds **zero** entries in these four states, so there are no HAVE marks; every candidate below is new ground for the research lanes to work down.
**Counts:** 168 candidates — 161 LEAD, 7 NO-ANCHOR? (Virginia 55: 53 LEAD / 2 NO-ANCHOR? · West Virginia 40: 38 / 2 · Maryland 45: 43 / 2 · Delaware 28: 27 / 1). 31 candidates carry a ★ THEME flag (prisons, asylums, hospitals, weird spots, military) and count double in lane priority. Counts verified against the table rows below on the date above.
**Lead sources used (leads only):** National Register of Historic Places and National Historic Landmark listings for VA/WV/MD/DE (NPS/Wikipedia state lists); National Park Service units in the four states (Colonial NHP, Richmond NBP, Harpers Ferry NHP, Antietam NB, Fort McHenry NMHS, Harriet Tubman Underground Railroad NHP, First State NHP, New River Gorge NPP, and others); state historic-site and state-park systems (Preservation Virginia, WV State Parks, Maryland State Parks / Maryland Historical Trust, Delaware State Parks / Delaware Division of Historical & Cultural Affairs); Clio miner-memorial and state tourism topic leads used as finders only.

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts.
**★ THEME** = the entry belongs to one of the five sweep families (prisons · asylums · hospitals · weird spots · military installations). Theme families count double in lane priority — flagged wherever they appear. Haunted history rides with several theme entries as labeled lore over a documented record, never as fact.

## Sequencing notes

- **Virginia opens most naturally at the Historic Triangle** (Jamestown–Williamsburg–Yorktown, eight LEADs within a few miles) and Richmond; the Shenandoah and Southwest lists can run as second-depth passes.
- **West Virginia's center of gravity is the coalfields:** Matewan, Welch, Hawks Nest, and the disaster memorials form one continuous labor corridor and should be researched as a connected set, not isolated entries. Blair Mountain stays flagged until lawful public ground is established.
- **Maryland's heaviest ground is Point Lookout and Crownsville** (theme families) and the Tubman landscape on the Eastern Shore; both want the reflection posture from the first draft.
- **Delaware is small but dense:** the First State NHP cluster (Wilmington–New Castle–Dover–Lewes) plus Fort Delaware and Fort Miles give the state a complete first pass in under thirty candidates.
- **Hawaii note:** none of these four states is Hawaii; per the owner's standing ruling Hawaii is a fly-in variant outside the drivable-49 count and is not part of this sweep.
