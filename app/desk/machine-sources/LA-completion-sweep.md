# Louisiana Completion Sweep — On Old Ground lead list

**Date:** November 20, 2026
**Purpose:** Checklist of significant visitable Louisiana historic ground NOT yet covered, so the research lane works down a list instead of building one.
**Counts:** 111 candidates — 97 LEAD, 14 NO-ANCHOR?
**Lead sources used (leads only):** Louisiana Office of State Parks state historic sites list (statutory list, La. R.S. 56:1685, and lastateparks.com); National Register / National Historic Landmark listings for Louisiana (NPS finding aid and NHL list, 54 Louisiana NHLs); Wikipedia lists (List of Louisiana state historic sites; List of National Historic Landmarks in Louisiana); Louisiana State Museum property list; NPS unit pages (Cane River Creole NHP, Jean Lafitte NHP&P, New Orleans Jazz NHP).

**LEADS ONLY — nothing here is researched copy.** Names, locations, and topics are a checklist so no ground is missed. Every entry that advances gets researched from primary and independent sources and written as an original dossier. No wording or photographs are taken from any lead source.

**Already covered — excluded from this list (8 filed dossiers):** Texas & Pacific Depot (Natchitoches), Front Street mercantile district (Natchitoches), Fort St. Jean Baptiste replica, Northwest Louisiana History Museum, Oakland Plantation, Magnolia Plantation, Los Adaes, Fort Jesup. The El Camino Real de los Tejas route draft is also filed.

**Marks:** LEAD = a lawful public anchor likely exists (anchor type named). NO-ANCHOR? = significant documented ground but no known lawful public anchor — flagged, not dropped; the researcher confirms or converts.
