## 2. The story

As East Louisiana State Hospital at Jackson grew severely overcrowded at the turn of the twentieth century, the Louisiana Legislature established a second state psychiatric hospital in **1906** on high pine-ridge ground overlooking Lake Buhlow and the Red River at **242 West Shamrock Street** in **Pineville**—originally known as the Louisiana Hospital for the Insane at Pineville and later **Central Louisiana State Hospital (CLSH)**. [1, 2]

For **118 years (`1906–2024`)**, the Shamrock Street grounds expanded into a large self-contained institutional campus with brick wards, administrative halls, and an agricultural/dairy complex (including a **1927 gambrel-roofed Dairy Barn** recorded in historic preservation files). In the 2020s, aging century-old infrastructure prompted the Louisiana Department of Health (**LDH**) Office of Behavioral Health to partner with neighboring Pinecrest Supports and Services Center on a **$33 million** replacement complex at **6250 Esler Field Road**—constructing two new patient-care buildings and a utility plant alongside three extensively renovated former Pinecrest buildings. [1, 2]

In **June 2024**, CLSH staff completed the move of all inpatient residents from Shamrock Street to Esler Field Road in a single day, and LDH held the official ribbon-cutting on **March 6, 2025** (116 inpatient psychiatric beds across Male and Female Services). Meanwhile, reuse proposals (including a senior-living community) were reported for the vacated Shamrock Street grounds. [1, 2, 3]
